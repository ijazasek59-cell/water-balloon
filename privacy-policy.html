Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SplashZone. We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit our website.

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors who browse our website, explore water balloon products or contact us with questions.

2. Information We May Collect

When you interact with our website, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, phone number and message details through our contact form.

Basic technical information, such as browser type, device information and general website usage data, may also be collected if analytics or similar services are enabled.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To respond to customer questions and inquiries.
  • To provide information about our products.
  • To improve website content and user experience.
  • To communicate about requests made by visitors.
  • To maintain website security and prevent misuse.

4. Contact Form Information

Information entered into a contact form should only be used for the purpose described when submitting it. Our actual information handling practices depend on how the form is connected to a backend or email service.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card details or other highly sensitive personal information through ordinary contact forms.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device to support website functionality and remember preferences. Our website may use cookies if these features are enabled.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use external services such as image hosting, analytics or contact form providers. Those services have their own privacy policies.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any external websites they visit.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through our website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. Parents and guardians should supervise children's internet activities.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information.

You may contact us to ask questions about information associated with your inquiry.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, business practices or applicable legal requirements change. Updated information will be published on this page.

12. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our information handling practices, please contact us.
